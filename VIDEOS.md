Each was chosen from web-search results, checked with YouTube's oEmbed service (exists, embedding allowed, real title and channel), played in YouTube's embedded player from the app, and judged against the app's own technique notes and frames from the video, with an independent challenge of every pick. No one watched every video in full, so the "Check" notes say what to look at when you review them. The runner-up is the judge's second choice: to use it instead, copy its id into `content/videos.json`.
